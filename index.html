Blink Analysis Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Blink Analysis is a research and educational tool that uses the iPhone front camera to measure blink characteristics. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, rule out, or treat any medical condition.

Information the App Uses

During a blink test, the app may create and store:

  • Four short front-camera video recordings used for blink analysis.
  • Derived blink measurements such as blink rate, eyelid closure, blink duration, timing, velocity, symmetry, and tracking-quality information.
  • Test-context information entered by the user, such as age, sex for reference-data analysis, recent sleep, time since waking, sleepiness, contact-lens use, and recent eye-drop use.
  • Research/data acknowledgments made before testing.

How Information Is Processed

Blink analysis is performed on the user's device. The current version of Blink Analysis does not automatically transmit session videos, test-context answers, or derived blink measurements to a Blink Analysis research database or server.

Local Storage

Session videos, analysis results, test-context information, and acknowledgment records are stored locally in the app's storage on the user's device.

Exporting Data

Users may deliberately choose to export session files using the app's export feature. When a user exports files, iOS allows the user to choose where those files are saved or sent. Copies exported outside Blink Analysis are then governed by the privacy practices of the destination selected by the user.

Research Contribution

The current public build does not automatically upload or contribute raw videos or derived blink measurements for research. If a future version introduces an optional research-contribution feature, Blink Analysis will provide a separate consent process and update this Privacy Policy before that feature is used.

Deleting Local Data

Users can delete locally stored Blink Analysis session data from within the app by opening Privacy & Data and selecting Delete All Local Session Data. This permanently deletes locally stored Blink Analysis session folders, including session recordings, analysis files, test-context information, and saved acknowledgment records.

Camera Access

Camera access is required to record the user's eyes during a blink test. Camera footage is used to perform blink analysis and is not automatically transmitted by the current version of the app.

Third-Party Services

Blink Analysis may use software libraries required to operate app functionality. The app does not intentionally use third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking services in the current version.

Children and Minors

Blink Analysis is intended as a research and informational measurement tool. The current public version does not automatically enroll users in a research study or upload their session data to a research database.

Medical Disclaimer

Blink measurements can be affected by factors including lighting, framing, eyelid visibility, camera position, frame rate, fatigue, contact-lens use, and other conditions. The measurements provided by Blink Analysis have not been validated as clinical diagnostic tests. Results should not be used as a substitute for professional medical evaluation.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's data practices or features change. The updated policy will display a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent through the support contact listed on the Blink Analysis App Store page.
