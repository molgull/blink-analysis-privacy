Blink Analysis Support

Support for Blink Analysis on iPhone and iPad

Blink Analysis is a research and informational tool that measures blink characteristics using the device's front camera.

Having trouble completing a test?

  • Make sure your face is centered and clearly visible to the front camera.
  • Use even lighting and avoid very dark rooms or strong backlighting.
  • Keep the device steady and try not to move your head during recordings.
  • If tracking quality is poor, reposition the device and restart the test.
  • Keep Blink Analysis open while the recordings are being analyzed.

Results or measurements look unusual

Blink measurements can vary with lighting, camera position, eyelid visibility, fatigue, contact lenses, and other factors. Blink Analysis is not a diagnostic medical tool and does not classify results as normal or abnormal.

Deleting saved test data

Open Privacy & Data from the app's opening screen and choose Delete All Local Session Data. This permanently removes locally stored Blink Analysis session folders from the app on that device.

Privacy

The current version processes blink recordings on-device and does not automatically upload session videos or derived blink measurements to a Blink Analysis research database.
